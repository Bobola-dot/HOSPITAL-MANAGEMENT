 Hospital Admissions Analysis

 Overview

A Power BI data analysis project exploring **1,000 hospital admission records** to identify patterns in readmissions, patient risk, hospital stays, treatment costs, and departmental performance.

 Key Insights

- **October** had the highest readmission rate at **26.74%**.
- **Cardiology** recorded the most readmissions with **38 cases**.
- Patients staying **1–3 days** had a higher readmission rate (**31.58%**) than those staying longer.
- Patients aged **61+** had the highest readmission rate at **37.71%**.
- Higher treatment costs were **not clearly associated with better recovery outcomes**.
- **Oncology** was identified as a high-cost department requiring further review.

 Recommendations

- Prioritize follow-up care for patients aged **61+**.
- Review Cardiology's readmission patterns and discharge processes.
- Monitor patients discharged after shorter hospital stays.
- Evaluate treatment costs alongside patient outcomes.
- Review high-cost departments such as Oncology and Surgery.
